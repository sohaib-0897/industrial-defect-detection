# Dataset-derived figures and annotations

Kolektor Surface-Defect Dataset 2 (KSDD2) was provided and annotated by Kolektor Group and published by ViCoS Lab.

Dataset and dataset-derived example images, error contact sheets and Grad-CAM illustrations:
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
Figures resize original images and add annotation boxes, crops or model attention overlays. The original images remain in the ignored data directory.

Source: https://www2.vicos.si/resources/kolektorsdd2/

Citation: Jakob Božič, Domen Tabernik, Danijel Skočaj. “Mixed supervision for surface-defect detection:
from weakly to fully supervised learning.” *Computers in Industry*, 2021.

The official source directs commercial-use enquiries to Danijel Skočaj.
KSDD2 is a public substitute dataset; it was not supplied by Spark AI.
